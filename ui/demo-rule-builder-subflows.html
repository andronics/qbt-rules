qbt-rulesrule builder — subflows concept

How this view works

Every all / any / none group and every $ref is a collapsible subflow. Double-click a card — or click its ⤢ badge — to zoom into it as its own mini-canvas. Use the breadcrumb strip at the top of the canvas (or ⤴ Up one level) to back out.

Node language

Portal — double border + ⤢ badge. A group or $ref that contains more nodes. Click to enter.
Terminal — single border, no badge. A leaf condition or a single action step. Nothing further inside.
ALL — every child must match
ANY — at least one child matches
NONE — no child may match
condition leaf (terminal)
$ref — reusable subflow (portal)
action step (terminal)
rail — sibling conditions (unordered)
flow — ordered action chain (animated)

Inspector

Click any card to inspect it. Double-click a card with a ⤢ badge (or click the badge) to drill into its subflow.
